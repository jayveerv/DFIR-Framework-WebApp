What the held-out set shows

  • The development-set result (section 1) does not carry over: on unseen data the right technique was found in 22.2% of in-scope samples. Classic attack captures (DeepBlueCLI: PsExec, Mimikatz, PowerShell, log clearing) are detected well; EVTX-to-MITRE samples mostly rely on Security events the framework does not parse yet (for example 4738/4724/5136 account changes, 4656/4663 object access, 5140/5145 detail fields).
  • 1,681 records in a few files could not be decoded by the python-evtx library (a known NullTypeNode error); they are counted, not hidden.
  • The gaps are listed by technique above; closing them needs new parsers and rules, which must then be measured on yet another unseen set.
